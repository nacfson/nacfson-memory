1. Provider-Agnostic Core Topology, Canonical Data Model & Security

mem1 replaces isolated vendor memories with a single cloud- and AI-agnostic control/data plane. Every agent connects through standardized interfaces, persists canonical records encrypted under tenant/user keys, and reads compiled cross-agent knowledge without binding storage or retrieval to a single model vendor.

Layer 1: Hard Tenant & Cryptographic Isolation
  • Mandatory Partition Key: Verified JWT/OAuth tenant_id binds every SQL query, Postgres RLS policy, Valkey hash tag ({t:<tenant_id>:ns:<ns>}), NATS subject, Qdrant is_tenant: true filter, and S3 prefix.
  • Envelope Encryption & Sub-DEKs: AES-256-GCM payloads store key_version (4B) || nonce_96 (12B) || ciphertext || tag (16B) with AAD = tenant_id || memory_id || version (CSPRNG capped at 232 writes/epoch). User-private records & S3 sessions/ use per-user/record sub-DEKs so GDPR hard_purge crypto-shreds without harming workspace memory.
  • Search Encryption Trade-Off: Decoupled Topology B/C keeps Postgres WAL encrypted and unwraps DEKs in projection workers (volume-TDE + purge receipts); consolidated in-Postgres pg_search requires plaintext/TDE-only searchable columns in WAL.
Layer 2: Soft Sharing, Scrubbing & Agnostic Abstractions
  • ReBAC Visibility Scopes: private:user (all user's agents), agent:only, workspace:shared (Cursor + ChatGPT + Grok on a project), and team:shared / org:shared, paired with least-privilege scopes (mem:read|write|extract|delete|purge|share).
  • Inline Secret Scrubber: Deterministic high-entropy regex + Aho-Corasick + NER redacts API keys/JWTs/passwords into [REDACTED:*] before NATS or external LLM calls.
  • Pluggable AI & Cloud Interfaces: 4 vendor-neutral AI interfaces (ExtractorProvider, EmbedderProvider, timeout-bounded RerankerProvider <= 120 ms → RRF, ConsolidatorProvider) over portable OCI/K8s, Postgres, Qdrant, Valkey, NATS, Temporal, S3, KMS/Vault, and OTLP.

3.2 Five Distributed Concurrency & Consistency Guarantees (Zero Distributed 2PC)

1. Shard Co-Location: Postgres partitions, Valkey slot tag {t:<tenant>:ns:<ns>}, NATS subjects, and Qdrant shard keys all partition by (tenant_id, memory_namespace).
2. Strong Ledger + Encrypted Outbox: Mutations commit atomically to Postgres; NATS relays encrypted payloads (zero plaintext in outbox) to idempotent workers (incoming_version > stored_version).
3. Contiguous-Watermark RYOW & Failover: Valkey tracks 53-bit composite (leader_epoch << 40) | seq_id (TTL >= 300 s). projected_seq advances strictly from Postgres outbox_events—never guessing from empty Valkey—with deterministic Postgres fallback on cold restart or async primary failover.
4. Fenced Semantic Dedup + Fast Counter TX: Namespace-scoped Valkey dedup_lease + cold-hydrated dedup_fence serialize Store+Extract. Step 4 runs an atomic Lua ownership/TTL check before opening the Postgres TX, updates namespace_counters (<1 ms lock hold) before the 3 inserts, and Step 5 updates RYOW/pending_emb monotonically (w > projected_seq, incoming_w > stored_w).
5. Multi-Cloud HLC, Contradictions & Backpressure: Server-assigned HLC (wall_ms, logical, node_id) defeats ±300 s client clock skew; conflicting claims emit CONTRADICTS edges for Dream; Valkey token buckets + NATS lag shedding (429 at lag > 10,000) protect hot Search.

5 Anti-Leakage Fairness Rules (Mandatory on Every Tier 4 Run)

(1) Public Surface Only: Ingest/query strictly through live REST/gRPC/MCP APIs. (2) 4-Stage Pinning & Variance Control: Pin exact snapshots (temp = 0.0) for Extractor, Consolidator, Reader, and Judge LLMs; evaluate 3-seed majority voting + paired McNemar's test (p < 0.05) to absorb ~±1–2% batching jitter. (3) Strict Token Cap: Enforce max_tokens <= 2,000 and top_k <= 15 (no brute-force stuffing). (4) Deterministic Retrieval Metrics: Track Recall@5/10 & NDCG@10 alongside LLM Judge.
